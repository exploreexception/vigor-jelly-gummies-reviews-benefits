Therefore, claims based on testimonials or general descriptions need to be separated from evidence demonstrating a measurable clinical effect.

Possible side effects will be shaped by the ingredients and the individual. Individuals may have gastrointestinal upset or other reactions to botanical extracts and amino acids. Consumers should also be wary of products that make broad claims about sexual performance, hormone levels, disease treatment or fast bodily changes. A dietary supplement should not be anticipated to deliver the same predictable and clinically established effects as an approved prescription medication.
